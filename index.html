Accounting / Terminology

Payments & reconciliation

01
19 · FOUNDATION

Status terminology used in the 19 training.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

“In Process” becomes “Paid”; the former “Paid” becomes “Reconciled”.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Read payment progress using the new labels.

The renamed states distinguish a registered payment from one that has been reconciled. This matters when users check whether money has merely been recorded or matched with the bank transaction. The wording in an older payment tutorial may therefore differ from the payment record shown in Odoo 20.

Check the payment record and its bank matching together. Invoice banners belong to a related workflow, so do not assume that every invoice status has been renamed in the same way.

Accounting / Workflow

Vendor bill matching

02
19 · FOUNDATION

Purchase order, receipt and bill matching remain the foundation.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Improved matching warnings, summaries and an option to unmatch bills from purchase orders.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

See what matched before approving a vendor bill.

The matching screen makes the relationship between a supplier bill and purchase order easier to inspect. Existing bill lines can be matched with order lines, and differences in quantities or prices are highlighted. The matching summary helps explain what is linked before the accountant posts or pays the bill.

A matching percentage is not proof that a bill is commercially correct. Review the quantities received, agreed prices and relevant bill-control policy before accepting a difference.

Accounting / Accounting flow

Inventory valuation

03
19 · FOUNDATION

The major inventory-accounting redesign already began in 19.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Closing accruals directly affect inventory valuation; later cost changes can update delivery and invoice costs.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Trace cost changes after goods have moved.

A delivery is not always the last event that determines its final cost. A later supplier price difference or landed cost can change the valuation of goods that have already been delivered or consumed. Odoo 20 also changes how perpetual-accounting accruals from the closing report affect inventory valuation.

For a useful comparison, follow the receipt, bill, delivery and later adjustment together. Check the affected valuation and accounting records rather than assuming the original delivery cost remains unchanged.

Operations / Posting & payments

Point of Sale

04
19 · FOUNDATION

Keep cash control, payment methods and reconciliation foundations.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Session closing uses a global sale; payment entries follow configured timing. Multicurrency checkout and inventory-free stock management are added.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Separate session closing from payment posting.

Session closing and payment posting need to be reviewed separately. The session produces a consolidated sale, while payment journal entries can follow their configured schedule without waiting for the cashier to close the session.

When comparing an older closing lesson, inspect the resulting sale and payment entries. Also distinguish simplified stock management from a setup that uses Inventory transfers.

Operations / Stock operations

Inventory & replenishment

05
19 · FOUNDATION

Reordering rules, forecast reports, routes and traceability remain core concepts.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

One replenishment Order button; suggested min/max levels; simplified returns; product-specific landed costs.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Review the calculated quantity, not just the button.

Replenishment still depends on forecasted stock and the product’s rules. The consolidated Order action changes the interface, while suggested stock levels provide a starting point for reviewing coverage. Routes, minimum and maximum quantities, pack multiples and lead times still affect the result.

Before ordering, check the quantity and destination. For returns and product-specific landed costs, follow the resulting stock and valuation records instead of expecting the older wizard or receipt-wide cost allocation.

Operations / Vendor management

Purchase

06
19 · FOUNDATION

Retain RFQ, alternative quote, receipt and control-policy knowledge.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Vendor-default Incoterms, improved alternatives comparison, preserved original arrival dates and vendor quality indicators.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Keep supplier terms and delivery promises visible.

Vendor defaults reduce repeated entry of purchasing terms. The arrival-date change also separates an updated operational expectation from the original supplier promise used to assess delivery performance.

Review the default Incoterm and both dates when comparing quotations or following a late order. Changing an expected arrival date should not be confused with the supplier having met the original commitment.

Operations / Production flow

Manufacturing

07
19 · FOUNDATION

Keep bills of materials, operations, consumption and valuation fundamentals.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Continuous production; flexible consumption by default; procurement can create draft or confirmed manufacturing orders.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Move finished quantities to the next operation sooner.

With Continuous Production enabled on the bill of materials, registering a partial quantity on one work order can unblock the next operation for that quantity. The next work center does not have to wait until the entire manufacturing quantity has passed through the first operation.

For example, the first 30 units of a 120-unit batch can advance while the remaining 90 continue upstream. This does not mean finished goods enter stock immediately: the documented flow places them in stock when the manufacturing order is completed.

Commercial / Pricing & orders

Sales & pricing

08
19 · FOUNDATION

Keep quotation-to-delivery-to-invoice reasoning.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Description-only lines, editable margins, periodic/packaging pricing and fulfillment without Inventory.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Check the context behind a quotation price.

A quotation’s price can depend on the selected pricelist, product or variant, quantity, packaging and validity period. Odoo 20 adds more ways to express pricing and edit order lines, so copying a price from an older tutorial is not enough to verify the result.

Review which rule actually applies and whether the line requires a product. For fulfillment without Inventory, check that flow separately from a warehouse-based delivery process.

Commercial / Pricing & scheduling

Rental & subscriptions

09
19 · FOUNDATION

Reuse rental periods, orders and recurring billing concepts.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Unified pricing; rental-period changes update prices; working schedules replace unavailable days. Subscription loyalty programs are added.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Recheck the price when a rental quotation changes.

Rental pricing now sits in the shared pricing framework. On an editable rental quotation, changing the rental period or pricelist can update existing line prices. Rental price conditions can also depend on the rental duration and the quantity rented, not just the product’s ordinary sales price.

Working schedules replace the old unavailable-day approach. Review the resulting price and permitted timing together; the separate subscription loyalty addition does not mean every existing subscription automatically receives a reward.

Operations / Cross-company flow

Intercompany flows

10
19 · FOUNDATION

Retain distinct-company accounting and warehouse configuration concepts.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Routes can resupply across companies; linked order updates and transferred valuation costs are improved.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Follow the stock and cost across company boundaries.

A warehouse can be resupplied from another company’s warehouse when access, shared products and routes are configured appropriately. A sales order can also use another selected company’s warehouse for delivery. The resulting moves belong to the relevant companies rather than becoming a single-company warehouse transfer.

Check the supplying company, receiving company, linked documents and valuation trail. This distinction matters because moving stock between two warehouses in one company is a different scenario from moving stock between legal entities.

Commercial / Lead management

CRM

11
19 · FOUNDATION

Reuse pipeline, contacts and opportunities.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

Lead rotation modes and subscription upsell/renewal links are expanded.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Connect lead allocation with the next sales action.

The rotation options make a team member’s participation in automatic assignment more explicit: always in rotation, in rotation with a limit, or out of rotation. Assignment still needs to be understood alongside the team’s matching rules and how automatic assignment is triggered.

The subscription links are a separate improvement: an opportunity can connect to an existing subscription and its upsell or renewal quotation. Check those links before creating an unrelated new sales process.

Productivity / Terminology

AI terminology

12
19 · FOUNDATION

AI features already existed in 19.

20 · WHAT CHANGES

AI Topics are renamed Skills.

THE CHANGE EXPLAINED

Map the old Topics terminology to Skills.

When an older lesson refers to an AI Topic, look for the corresponding Skills configuration in Odoo 20. Skills group the instructions and tools that define what an agent can do, while sources provide reference information. The terminology change does not make every agent capable of every task.

The Odoo 20 documentation states that an agent without assigned skills can provide information but cannot perform database tasks. Review the actual skills and tools attached to the agent before expecting it to carry out an operation.
